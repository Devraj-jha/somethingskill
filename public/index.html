How SkillPath works

Four steps from “where am I?” to “what should I learn next?”.

1. Record your skills

Rate yourself from level 0 to 3 on technical and soft skills.

2. Pick a career

Compare your profile with the expectations for a role.

3. See your gaps

A transparent weighted score shows what you have, what needs work and what is missing.

4. Follow your roadmap

Get resources and projects matched to your gaps and track progress.

Why skill-gap assessment matters

Graduates often study broadly while employers hire for specific competencies. Seeing the difference early lets you spend limited time on what counts. SkillPath’s score is guidance for planning, not a prediction of employment.
